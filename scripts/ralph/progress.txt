# Ralph Progress Log - Descobrir: Busca Avancada, Filtros e Ordenacao

## Codebase Patterns
- `com.app.media`: novo record publico `MediaPage(results, page, totalResults, totalPages)` e o contrato de paginacao padrao; reutilize-o em qualquer endpoint paginado (trending, discover, search) em vez de criar DTOs de pagina paralelos.
- TMDB `/trending/all/week` devolve itens no mesmo formato de `/search/multi` (mesmos campos `media_type`, `title`/`name`, `poster_path`, etc.) — reaproveite `TmdbMultiSearchItem` para o mapeamento por item; so foi necessario um record novo (`TmdbTrendingResponse`) para os campos extras `page`/`total_pages`/`total_results`.
- Sandbox deste ambiente Ralph NAO tem JDK/Maven nem acesso a rede (Maven Central bloqueado), entao `./mvnw test` nao pode ser executado aqui — build so pode ser verificado onde JDK 21 + Maven Central estao disponiveis (ver nota em api/CLAUDE.md). Revisar o codigo manualmente com cuidado antes de commitar.

- `MediaPage`'s compact constructor clamps `totalPages`/`totalResults` to the TMDB cap (500 pages, 10000 results) — applies automatically to every construction site, so new paginated endpoints (search, discover) don't need to reimplement the clamp.
- TMDB `/discover/movie` and `/discover/tv` item shapes differ from `/search/multi`/`/trending/all/week` (no `media_type`, but include `popularity`) — use the dedicated `TmdbDiscoverItem`/`TmdbDiscoverResponse` records (not `TmdbMultiSearchItem`) for anything backed by `/discover/*`, and pass the known `MediaType` explicitly (it's not on the item) when mapping to `MediaSearchResult`.
- To test the query params/path a service builds for a TMDB call, capture the `Function<UriBuilder, URI>` passed to `.uri(...)` with an `ArgumentCaptor<Function<UriBuilder, URI>>` (`ArgumentCaptor.forClass(Function.class)` + `@SuppressWarnings("unchecked")`), then apply it to `UriComponentsBuilder.newInstance()` and inspect `UriComponentsBuilder.fromUri(uri).build().getQueryParams()` — this is the pattern for any future "assert the exact TMDB URL built" test.
